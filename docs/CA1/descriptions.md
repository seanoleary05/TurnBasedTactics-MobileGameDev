# Turn Based Tactics - Game Description & Features

## Game Description

**Turn Based Tactics** is a single-player tactical strategy game where players control a small team of units through challenging combat encounters. Success depends on careful planning, positioning, and resource management, as every decision can impact the outcome of future battles. Players must adapt to changing encounter layouts and enemy behaviour while completing missions as efficiently as possible to maximise their rewards.

The game is designed to create feelings of tension and relief, rewarding strategic thinking and punishing careless mistakes. Each battle presents a tactical puzzle where understanding enemy priorities and managing your party effectively are the keys to victory.

**Offline play only. No advertisements. No in-app purchases.**

---

## Features (In Order of Importance)

### 1. Turn-Based Battle System
- Tactical combat between the player's party and enemy units.
- Players can move units and use abilities during their turn.
- Battles are won through strategic positioning and decision-making.

### 2. Enemy Action Priority System
- Enemies follow a defined targeting and action priority system.
- Players can predict and plan around enemy behaviour.
- Creates meaningful tactical decisions during encounters.

### 3. Party and Inventory Management
- Players must manage available resources between missions.
- Choices made in one battle affect future encounters.
- Encourages long-term strategic planning.

### 4. Pseudo-Random Encounter Generation
- Enemy and player starting positions are generated using a pseudo-random system.
- Increases replayability and requires adaptation each run.
- Prevents encounters from feeling identical.

### 5. Save and Load System
- Players can save progress and continue later.
- Supports mobile play sessions and testing.

### 6. Basic Enemy AI
- Enemies make decisions based on simple tactical rules.
- Supports movement and attack choices during combat.
- Provides an adequate challenge for the vertical slice.
